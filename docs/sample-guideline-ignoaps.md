Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS), a component of the National Social Assistance Programme, Ministry of Rural Development.

Eligibility: The applicant must be aged 60 years or above and must belong to a household living below the poverty line (BPL) according to the criteria prescribed by the Government of India.

Central assistance: Rs. 200 per month for beneficiaries aged 60 to 79 years, and Rs. 500 per month for beneficiaries aged 80 years and above. State governments are urged to contribute an additional amount.

Documents generally required: Aadhaar card (age proof), BPL proof, bank account passbook.
