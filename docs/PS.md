# Cline AI Builder Hackathon: Round 2 Problem Statement

India has problems that millions of people live with every day. What would you build with AI to solve one of them?

India is building at a scale few countries have ever attempted, yet for hundreds of millions of people daily life still runs into the same walls. A farmer cannot tell what is wrong with his crop until the damage is done. A patient from a small town travels for hours to meet a doctor who has only a few minutes to give her. A student studies from notes written in a language she does not think in. A citizen loses months to a government scheme he qualified for but never heard about.

These are not rare cases. They repeat across healthcare, education, agriculture, governance, finance, safety and climate, and across languages, incomes and literacy levels. AI can finally make a real dent: it understands Indian languages and voice, reads messy documents, reasons over local data and can reach anyone with a basic smartphone.

**The challenge:** pick one real problem that Indian society faces and, in 8 hours, build an AI-powered solution for it, using Cline as your AI coding partner from the first commit to the last. You can also build Cline into the solution itself, for example with the Cline SDK. Not a slide deck, but a working product that a real person could use tomorrow.

The problem is yours to choose. Healthcare, education, agriculture, governance, finance, safety and climate are only some examples, so look at the problems around you and pick one that matters. For example, potholes are a problem on roads across India, and one solution is a dashcam in a car with AI that spots potholes as it drives and prepares the complaint automatically.

## Deliverables
- **Codebase on GitHub (with README):** a public repository with a clear README covering the problem, your solution, architecture, tech stack, setup steps and how Cline was used in the build.
- **PPT:** a presentation covering the problem, your solution, the architecture, the AI models and tools involved, how Cline was used (in the build and inside the solution), challenges faced and future scope.
- **Demo video (mandatory):** a short walkthrough of your working solution with a real user scenario, showing Cline in action during the build.
- **Social media post (brownie points):** share your build, demo, experience or participation shoutout, and tag Cline.

## Evaluation criteria
Creativity & uniqueness · Innovation · Real-world usefulness · Effective use of Cline · Product usability & overall execution · Technical implementation. **Bonus** for solutions that use Cline inside the product itself, not only to build it.
