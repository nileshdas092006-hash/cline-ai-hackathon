# Research summary (for README and slides)

## Why not a "scheme finder"
Saturated: the government's myScheme portal already finds schemes, and Microsoft + AI4Bharat launched Jugalbandi, a voice WhatsApp scheme bot in Indian languages, in 2023 (https://techcrunch.com/2023/05/24/microsoft-ai-chatgpt-reaches-rural-india/).

## The real gap: people lose benefits AFTER applying
- **Rajasthan, PM-KISAN (2019):** only about 23% of about 51 lakh registered farmers received the third instalment; names on applications did not match Aadhaar. Under 1% were paid in Ganganagar district. Source: Hindustan Times, https://www.pressreader.com/india/hindustan-times-st-jaipur/20191007/281586652343870
- **Andhra Pradesh, PM-KISAN (LibTech India, Jan 2022):** about 15.2 lakh farmers missed instalments, about ₹1,343 crore pending; 47% bank rejections, 51% "action required by the state" with no reason given; only 12.5% of 33,526 studied cases were resolved, some pending for 1,060+ days. Source: https://libtech.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/‘PM-KISAN-Samman-Nidhi-AP-25th-January.pdf
- **Documents as the gate (Haqdarshak):** a cited study attributes 20% of beneficiary exclusion to documentation issues; 45% of surveyed drivers had trouble submitting forms; middlemen charged ₹150–300 for a card priced at ₹35. Advocacy source. https://yojanacard.haqdarshak.com/?p=9509

Present these as documented examples (old and regional), not current national totals.

## Pitch
"He qualified. He applied. He got nothing, because of one letter in his name. KaagazCheck catches that before he applies."
